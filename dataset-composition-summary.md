## Dataset Composition

### Scope

Fully synthetic dataset modeling an optical networking equipment manufacturer's demand planning environment. Covers an **18-month horizon** (Jan 2025 – Jun 2026) across **5 products × 30 customers**, producing ~40k daily order records and a parameterized supply-demand simulation.

---

### Dimension Tables

| Table | Rows | Key Attributes |
|-------|------|----------------|
| **products** / **dim_product** | 5 | `product_id`, `product_name`, `product_type` (Transponder, Line Card, ROADM, Pluggable), `speed_gbps`, `lifecycle_stage` (NPI / Active / EOL), `launch_date`, `eol_date`, `lead_time_days` (14–90d), `base_demand`, `lifecycle_curve`, `segment_multiplier`, `bias_factor` |
| **customers** | 30 | `customer_id`, `customer_name`, `segment` (Tier-1 Carrier, Cloud Provider, Regional ISP, Data Center, Enterprise), `region` (North America, EMEA, APAC, Global), `customer_multiplier` (0.7–2.0× by segment) |
| **pricing** | 5 | `product_id`, `list_price`, `discount_pct`, `net_price` |

### Customer Segments & Demand Multipliers

| Segment | Count | Multiplier | Behavioral Pattern |
|---------|-------|------------|-------------------|
| Tier-1 Carrier | 7 | 1.5× | Quarter-end surges (+80%), concentrated large orders |
| Cloud Provider | 6 | 2.0× | Mid-month peaks (+30%), 24-hour ordering, even order sizes |
| Regional ISP | 6 | 0.7× | Flat intra-month, business-hours only |
| Data Center | 4 | 1.3× | Early-month front-loading (+40%) |
| Enterprise | 6 | 1.0× | Monday-heavy (+40%), few large + many small orders |

### Demand Generation Pipeline

**Monthly demand** is computed per product × customer as:

$$\text{demand} = \text{base\_demand} \times \text{lifecycle\_curve} \times \text{seasonality} \times \text{customer\_multiplier} \times \text{noise}$$

Where:
- **Lifecycle curves** vary by product type and stage: NPI ramps from 20 units (+5/mo), Active holds ~120–150, EOL declines from 60 (−3/mo)
- **Seasonality** follows quarterly multipliers: Q1 = 0.85, Q2 = 1.05, Q3 = 1.10, Q4 = 1.20
- **Noise** is uniform ±5%

**Daily order explosion** distributes monthly demand across calendar days using:
- Day-of-week weighting (Tue–Thu heaviest, weekends near-zero)
- Segment-specific intra-month patterns (see table above)
- Month-end uplift (+20% after the 25th)
- 15–20 orders per product × customer × month
- Segment-specific order-size concentration (e.g., Tier-1 carriers: few large + many small)
- Segment-specific time-of-day stamps (e.g., cloud providers: bimodal 4–10am or 24/7)

---

### Forecast Generation

Forecasts are intentionally biased by lifecycle stage:

| Stage | Forecast Bias | Range |
|-------|--------------|-------|
| NPI | Under-forecast | 0.85–0.95× actual |
| Active | Slight under-forecast | 0.95–1.05× actual |
| EOL | Over-forecast | 1.10–1.25× actual |

---

### Supply Generation (Monthly Baseline)

Monthly supply commitment varies by lifecycle:

| Stage | Supply Ratio | Interpretation |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| NPI | 0.6–0.8× demand | Constrained ramp |
| Active | 1.0–1.1× demand | Near-balanced |
| EOL | 1.5–1.8× demand | Excess inventory drawdown |

Decomposed as `on_hand` (70% of total) and `inbound_supply` (20% of total), with `supply_commit` representing the full supply amount.

---

### Parameterized Supply-Demand Simulation

The daily `mart_supply_demand` / `combined_df` output is generated by a configurable pipeline with the following default scenario:

**Scenario Parameters:**

| Parameter | Default | Description |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| `HORIZON_START` / `END` | 2025-01-01 – 2026-06-30 | Simulation window |
| `BASE_SUPPLY_FACTOR` | 0.95 | Pre-shock supply as fraction of forecast |
| `SHOCK_START` / `END` | Jul 1–31, 2025 | Supply disruption window |
| `SHOCK_FACTOR` | 0.40 | Supply drops to 40% during shock |
| `SPIKE_START` / `END` | Oct 1–14, 2025 | Demand surge window |
| `SPIKE_FACTOR` | 1.35 | +35% demand during spike |
| `DAYS_OF_SUPPLY_OPENING` | 30 | Opening inventory = 30 days of avg demand |
| `LEADTIME_MIN/MAX` | 10–25 days | Random lead-time offset on supply |

**Time-Varying Supply Factor Schedule:**

| Period | Factor | Phase |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Pre-Jul 2025 | 0.95× | Baseline |
| Jul 2025 (shock onset) | 1.00× | Maximize diminished capacity |
| Aug 15 – Nov 2025 | 1.10× | Leadership ramp |
| Dec 2025 – Jan 2026 | 1.20× | Recovery boost |
| Feb – Mar 2026 | 1.00× | Taper to match demand |
| Apr 2026 onward | 0.95× | Return to baseline |

**Pipeline Steps:**
1. Generate date spine over horizon
2. Apply demand spike to raw orders
3. Compute 14-day moving average forecast
4. Multiply forecast by time-varying supply factor → planned supply
5. Apply random lead-time offset (10–25 days)
6. Apply shock factor to supply arriving during shock window
7. Union with opening inventory (day-1 stock = 30 days of avg demand)
8. Full outer join demand and supply on date × product over a complete spine
9. Compute cumulative demand, cumulative supply, backorder qty, inventory position

**Output columns** (`combined_df` / `mart_supply_demand`): `date`, `product_id`, `demand_qty`, `supply_qty`, `cumulative_demand`, `cumulative_supply`, `backorder_qty`, `inventory_position`
